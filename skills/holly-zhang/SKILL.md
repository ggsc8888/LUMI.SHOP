Personality: quiet confidence, precise and honest, warm and personal, classic with a modern twist.

## 1. Listing copy (title, description, SEO)

- Voice: calm, assured, warm. Short sentences. Don't use hype words (stunning, must-have, gorgeous, luxury, glamorous, opulent) or exclamation marks.
- Title: `[Pearl type] [form] [key detail], [metal]`, e.g. "South Sea Pearl Halo Ring, 18K Yellow Gold". State pearl size in mm when measured.
- Description structure:
  1. One quiet, evocative line about the piece and how it is worn or given (one moment: putting it on, touching it, giving it, at rest).
  2. Bulleted specs: pearl type, size (mm, inches in parentheses), shape, color/overtone, luster; metal and karat; stones; measurements; closure/findings; ring size.
  3. One care line ("Store separately; wipe with a soft cloth after wearing; keep away from perfume and hairspray.").
  4. Optional gift line ("Arrives in the Holly Zhang signature box.") only if the seller's settings confirm it.
- **Honest by default.** Never state as fact what is not visible or told: pearl origin (natural/cultured, Akoya/South Sea/Tahitian/freshwater), treatments, karat/hallmarks, diamond carat or natural vs lab-grown, certificates. If inferred, phrase it cautiously ("appears to be…") and add a verifyNotes item.
- Pearl colors are described truthfully: white stays white, Tahitian stays grey-green / peacock, golden stays gold. Never upgrade a color or grade in copy.
- SEO: meta title ≤ 60 chars, meta description ≤ 155 chars in the same voice; handle in lowercase-hyphen form; tags cover pearl type, form, metal, color, occasion (gift, bridal, everyday). Don't keyword-stuff.
- Collections/vocabulary that may appear in tags when the seller indicates them: Signatures, Bridal, Everyday essentials, Statement pieces, New arrivals, Zhang Eclipse, Tahitian, Men's & unisex, Sage, Evergreen.

## 2. Pricing

Position as fine pearl jewelry sold by a gallery (catalog median ≈ $960). Base suggestions on materials, pearl size/quality and the seller's settings. Never justify price with unverified claims. When grade, origin or metal is unverified, say the price assumes the seller confirms them.

## 3. Image direction (summary; full rules in references)

- **Primary product image ("White studio" / packshot):** square 1:1 (2048 × 2048 target), sRGB, **one solid background, HEX #EFEFEB / RGB 239,239,235, opaque**, corner to corner. No gradient, vignette, texture, horizon, colored bounce, props, text or watermark. One large soft light from the upper-left (≈45°), one soft highlight per pearl at 10–11 o'clock, only a faint contact shadow. Use the category placement card.
- **Never write "white background"** for product images. White (#FFFFFF) is for web page surfaces only.
- **On-model / scene / gift / campaign images** are *not* flat-filled. They follow Look A (window light) by default: one soft key from frame-left 45–70°, 5200–5500 K, pearl is the brightest point, skin half a stop darker, background darker still, solid-color clothing, at most one sea-green (#9CCBC9) object that never touches the pearl.
- The jewelry in every generated image must match the seller's real photos exactly: same pearl count, size relationships, color/overtone, metal, stones, settings and findings. Never add or remove parts, retouch away defects, or guess unseen backs/posts.
- Avoid words that pull the model off-brand: luxury, glamorous, opulent, sparkling, glitter, bokeh, dreamy, cinematic, 8k, HDR, golden hour, candlelight, warm glow, beach, tropical, turquoise, marble.

## 4. verifyNotes the agent should add when relevant

- Pearl type/origin and any treatments are unverified.
- Metal karat/hallmark is unverified.
- Diamond/stone carat weight and natural vs lab-grown are unverified.
- Ring size / length measured vs estimated.
- Any AI-generated image: check pearl count, size, color, metal and findings against the real piece before publishing. AI images are previews; see the AI image policy for where they may be used.
- Primary product image: confirm the background reads RGB 239,239,235 at all four corners. Text prompts alone do not guarantee exact color.
