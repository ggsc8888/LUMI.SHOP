# Campaign, on-model and scene direction

Sources: the Holly Zhang Photography Guide, Parts 1–2 (brand layer and Looks A/B/C) and the Holly Zhang Campaign Studio skill. These images are **not** flat-filled with #EFEFEB.

## Brand layer (fixed for every look)

- One soft light, area ≥ 3× the subject, key from **frame-left**, front-side 45–70°. No on-camera flash, bare flash, ring light or direct sun on the pearl.
- Brightness order: **pearl > skin > background.** Skin is 1/3–2/3 stop darker than the pearl highlight. One soft highlight per pearl at 10–11 o'clock, never clipped.
- The pearl is the hero; the person is "the one wearing it", not selling it. The pearl sits at center or on a third, in the brightest area, never covered by hair, fingers, collar or shadow. Earrings: hair fully behind the ear, earlobe toward the light. Necklaces: open neckline, pearls on the collarbone.
- Close-ups and macro show one piece only. Full-length shows at most 3 pieces of the same series and metal. Don't mix metal colors in one frame.
- Expression must have an object: a person, the window, their own hands, or the camera like an old friend. Three ranges: *confident* (full/half-length, soft direct gaze), *warm* (gifting, bridal, couples, real smiles at each other), *quiet* (close-ups: profile, eyes down or closed). Avoid commercial smiles, exaggerated surprise, pouting and cold stares.
- Hands do only four things: **put on, touch, hand over, rest.** No fists, pointing at the jewelry, or chin-on-hand posing. Short nails, nude polish, no unrelated rings or watches.
- Clothing: solid colors, no pattern, logo or sequins; contrast with the pearl (white pearls with neutral/dark; Tahitian with white, cream, or black in Look C). Natural makeup, no heavy red lip, no hats or sunglasses.
- Models: adults 28–60 (include 40+), Asian, white and other ethnicities, reflecting Bellevue/Seattle clients. Men 30–55 either give the piece or wear Tahitian.
- No filters, black-and-white, film grain or heavy grading. Never recolor a pearl. No text burned into photos. No watermarks.

## Looks (series layer: changes only setting, color temperature, contrast and wardrobe)

- **A · Window light (default for everything not listed below).** Gallery high window, pale walls, late-morning daylight 5200–5500 K, contrast 1:2–1:3, slightly cool shadows. Props: oat linen, sea-foam white wall, light wood, small walnut tray, signature gift box, handwritten card, a glass of water or porcelain dish; exactly one sea-green item (ribbon, scarf or glass). No flower piles, no marble.
- **B · Coast grey (Evergreen only).** Overcast Washington coast: wet basalt, driftwood, morning sea mist, cool. A large reflector under the face. Wool coats, cream knits. No starfish, shells or nets.
- **C · Nocturne (Zhang Eclipse, Tahitian, Men's, optional holiday).** Dark studio, one dark pearl plus one ring of light, contrast 1:6 or more, black silk shirt, tungsten accents. Keep shadow detail; don't let Tahitian overtone vanish.

## Jewel Lister scene presets

- **On model:** close-up, Look A. Adult wearer cropped at chin/ear/neck/hand as the piece needs. Window key from frame-left 45°, 5300 K, ratio 1:2. Solid ivory/oat/charcoal clothing. Hands only put on / touch / rest. Only this piece; no other jewelry. Generated faces/hands mean preview only.
- **Flat lay (scene still life):** oat washed linen, walnut or white-oak wood, or white porcelain; one small sea-green (#9CCBC9) object that does not touch the pearl; overcast window light from the left, 5300 K, cool grey soft shadows; 85 mm f/4; piece about one third of frame; large empty space. No flowers, perfume, silk folds or writing.
- **Gift box:** the Holly Zhang signature box open on a walnut surface, a blank handwritten-style card (no legible text), a sea-green satin ribbon; window light from the left; the piece sits in the box exactly as photographed; warm-but-neutral 5300 K, not orange.

## Prompt hygiene (Campaign Studio)

- One standalone image, never a collage or grid.
- Lock only visible, confirmable product traits: shape, material, color, pearl order, connections, relative scale, pendant, clasp. Unseen backs are not verified.
- Name exactly which product appears; never add necklaces, rings, bracelets or diamonds that aren't in the reference.
- Write lighting as executable direction: key direction and size, fill, negative fill, rim, highlight shape, shadow edge, background relationship.
- Natural skin texture, real hair, nacre depth, believable metal reflections, correct hands and gravity. Pearls are not glowing white balls.
- Text: none by default. If requested, a short line plus "Holly Zhang" / "PEARL GALLERY" only.
